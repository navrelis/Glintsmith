# Glintsmith

>>> PASTE IMAGE: banner.png | Glintsmith <<<

Item outlines, custom enchantment glints and a fix for the gaps in item models, for every item from every mod, in one Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.1.

Glintsmith works on what the game really draws, so there are no item whitelists and no per-item resource-pack overrides. A sword from a mod, a helmet, a shield, an elytra, a dropped stack and an item in a frame get the same treatment as vanilla tools. Set it up in one screen in the game, in English or German.

>>> PASTE IMAGE: showcase.gif | The looks you can set up: outline colours, glint presets, and rules <<<

## What it does

**Outlines for every item.** The outline is computed from the item's real rendered shape, so flat items, 3D models, shields, tridents, banners, armor and the items of other mods all get a clean outline of the same width. It is depth aware: where a wall or a block is in front of the item, the outline is hidden. <!-- VERIFY IN GAME: no outline through walls; works on items of advancednetherite, eternal_starlight, cataclysm, immersive_armors, and on shield, trident, banner, elytra -->

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_universal.png | Every item from every mod <<<

**Outlines that fit your look.** Colours can be static, rainbow, pulse, gradient (up to 16 colours) or taken from the enchantments on the item, with a colour of its own for every enchantment. Thickness (0.5 to 16 screen pixels), softness, opacity, distance fade and scaling with the window size are settings. A low-durability alert makes the outline pulse towards a warning colour when an item is nearly broken, and "Only my items" leaves other players' items alone.

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_outline_styles.png | Outlines that fit your look <<<

**Outlines wherever an item is drawn.** First-person hand, third-person held items of players and mobs, dropped items and item frames are on by default. Worn armor (this includes worn elytra) and items in the inventory and other menus are available too and are off by default. Each place has its own switch.

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_targets.png | Everywhere an item is drawn <<<

**Your own enchantment glint.** Four layers, items, armor, entities and the GUI, each with its own settings and each can be turned off. Pick the vanilla glint, one of the generated presets (rainbow, aurora, fire, frost, sparkle, gold, void), a PNG of your own from the config folder (`file:<name>.png`), or a texture from a resource pack. Add a tint, change speed, scale, strength and rotation. The tint can follow the enchantments of the item. The presets are generated by the mod, so no third-party art is included, and their brightness is kept near the vanilla glint: the glint is added on top of the item, so a texture that is bright everywhere would wash the item out. The game's own Glint Speed and Glint Strength sliders still count.

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_glint_presets.png | Pick your glint <<<

**A fix for gaps in item models.** Some flat item models show thin seams and gaps between their faces (Mojang bug MC-73186), which the vanilla glint leaves visible. Glintsmith scales the UV shrink on the block atlas, grows and moves the side faces of generated items, and can build the sides from contiguous pixel runs only. Set it to off, automatic or custom. Automatic turns itself off when the mod Model Gap Fix (`modelfix`) is installed, because that mod already closes the seams. Custom still applies next to it, on top of that mod's own changes. <!-- VERIFY IN GAME: gap fix visible effect on 16x and HD items; AUTO stays off with modelfix present (depends on the wiring task T8) -->

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_gap_fix.png | Seams closed, edges clean <<<

**Rules for exactly the items you pick.** A rule matches by item id, tag, mod, wildcard or negation, with an exception list, and optionally by enchantment, by enchanted or not, and by the place the item is drawn in. It can override the outline and the glint. Later rules override earlier ones, so a "deny" is a rule placed after the broader one. A fresh config contains two examples: a pale outline for the Totem of Undying, and no outline on enchanted books.

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_rules.png | Rules for exactly the items you pick <<<

**Everything in one screen.** The settings screen uses vanilla widgets and has tabs for outlines, glint, model fix, rules and enchantment colours, a colour picker, and a tooltip on every setting. It opens from Mod Menu, with `/glintsmith`, or with a key. The settings are one JSON file, `config/glintsmith.json`, which is read again while the game runs.

## Sodium and Iris

Glintsmith is made to run with Sodium and Iris. With Iris and no shader pack selected, outlines and glint work as in vanilla rendering. With a shader pack:

- Outlines in the world are turned off, because they need the scene depth that the shader pack changes.
- Outlines on the item in your hand are best effort and can be switched off ("Hand with shader pack").
- Outlines in the GUI work.
- "Off with shader packs" turns all outlines off while a pack is active.

<!-- VERIFY IN GAME: each Iris statement above; hand alignment with shader packs; glint under a shader pack (GUI only is customised); Sodium 0.8.13 and Fabulous graphics -->

>>> PASTE IMAGE: card_shaders.png | Sodium and Iris friendly <<<

If an error happens in the outline, glint or gap fix code, that feature switches itself off for the session and logs it once, and the game keeps rendering the vanilla way.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader, Java 21
- Fabric API
- Optional: Mod Menu (config button), Sodium, Iris

Client side only. Install it on your client; it does nothing on a server.

## Compatibility notes

- A mod that replaces the vanilla glint render types itself can win over Glintsmith's glint, or the other way round. Use one of them.
- Items that a mod draws without the game's normal item rendering are not outlined.
- Outlines and glint on items in the GUI work for items drawn by the game's own inventory code. Mods that batch their own GUI items may bypass this.
- Model Gap Fix (`modelfix`) can stay installed. Glintsmith's automatic mode stays off while it is present. In custom mode the settings stack on top of it, and precise spans are inactive.

## Commands and keys

- `/glintsmith` opens the settings.
- `/glintsmith reload` reads `config/glintsmith.json` again and lists repairs.
- `/glintsmith toggle` switches all outlines on or off.
- Two keys in the controls list, "Open Glintsmith settings" and "Toggle item outlines", both unbound by default.

Put your own glint PNGs in `config/glintsmith/glints/` (lower case names, up to 1024 x 1024 px).
